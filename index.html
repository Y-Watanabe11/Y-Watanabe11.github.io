03 / ABOUT

At the intersection of
physics and engineering.

I am a master's student in the Graduate School of Science and Technology at Tokyo University of Science. I am interested in developing instruments that make new observations possible, and in using careful data analysis to learn from those observations.

My interests span radiation detectors, analog and mixed-signal electronics, spacecraft instrumentation, high-energy astrophysics, and scientific software.
