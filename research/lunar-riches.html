01Overview

THE BIG PICTURE

Beyond the atmosphere, new questions.

Understanding charged particles in the space environment matters for both scientific measurements and future exploration. A detector must make sense of energetic particles arriving from many directions.

Lunar-RICHeS is a small particle-spectrometer development effort for the lunar environment. The research area includes silicon detector tracking and response analysis, connecting particle physics with space instrumentation.
