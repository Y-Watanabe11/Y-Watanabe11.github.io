01Overview

THE BIG PICTURE

Start with the signal.

A photodetector converts a physical event into an electrical signal. Readout electronics determine how much information can be recovered from that signal — and how reliably it can be measured.

SATORI focuses on a compact, low-power custom analog readout integrated circuit for silicon photomultipliers (SiPMs). It sits at the interface between detector physics and practical electronic instrumentation.
