01Overview

THE BIG PICTURE

The universe has a spectrum.

Some of the most energetic environments in the universe reveal themselves in X-rays. High-resolution spectroscopy turns that light into information about hot gas, elements and motion.

XRISM is an X-ray astronomy mission led by JAXA with contributions from NASA and ESA. My research interests include X-ray spectral analysis and reproducible workflows for interpreting astronomical observations.
